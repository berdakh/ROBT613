# Cooking notes

## Lentil soup (the reliable one)
Fry one chopped onion and two cloves of garlic in olive oil until soft, about
6 minutes. Add 1 teaspoon cumin and half a teaspoon paprika, stir for 30
seconds. Add 200 g red lentils, one tin of tomatoes and 1 litre of water.
Simmer 25 minutes until the lentils collapse. Salt at the *end* — salting early
makes lentils take longer to soften. Serves 4, costs about 900 KZT.

## Rice, properly
Ratio is 1 cup basmati to 1.5 cups water. Rinse the rice until the water runs
clear, otherwise it turns gluey. Bring to the boil, cover, lowest heat for 12
minutes, then leave it covered off the heat for 10 more. Do not lift the lid
during those 10 minutes.

## Things I keep getting wrong
- Frozen spinach must be squeezed dry or the dish turns watery.
- Chicken thighs need 25 minutes at 200°C, not 18. I have undercooked them
  three times now.
- Yoghurt curdles if added to a boiling pot. Take it off the heat first.

## Freezer rules
Cooked rice keeps 4 days in the fridge, 1 month frozen. Soup freezes well.
Anything with yoghurt or potatoes does not freeze well — the texture breaks.
