# Travel notes

## Almaty trip, planned for April
The train takes about 13 hours overnight and costs around 8,000 KZT in
platskart. Flying is 90 minutes but usually 25,000–40,000 KZT. The overnight
train saves a hotel night, which is why I keep choosing it.

## Packing list that I always forget half of
Charger, power bank, adapter, one warm layer (evenings are cold even in
April), refillable bottle, printed ticket as a backup, medication, and the
small notebook.

## Lessons from last time
- I underestimated food costs on the road; budget 4,000 KZT a day.
- The station has no reliable wifi. Download everything before leaving.
- Left the charger in the hostel. Now I count cables before checking out.
